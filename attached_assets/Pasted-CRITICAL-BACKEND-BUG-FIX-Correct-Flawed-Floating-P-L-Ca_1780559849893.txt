CONTEXT & BUG DESCRIPTION:

Before TP2 was hit, the active trade row displayed an inflated Floating P&L of ~$22 to ~$27. However, upon hitting TP2 and closing the remainder of the position, the final injected Realized P&L delta was exactly $14.31 (moving from +$4.44 to +$18.75), stabilizing the Equity at $218.75 instead of the anticipated ~$240+.
